# Macro Doctor Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Macro Doctor ("the app") is provided by minyong ("we").

## What the app does with your data
- The app reads the list of Confluence spaces and the content of pages in the space you choose to scan. It does this in your browser, using your own Confluence permissions.
- The app does not store page content, scan results or personal data. Results exist only in your browser tab and are gone when you close or reload it.
- The app does not send any data to servers operated by us or by third parties. It has no remote back end and declares no external network access.
- The app does not use cookies, analytics or tracking of its own.

## Data handled by Atlassian
The app runs on Atlassian Forge. Atlassian hosts the app and may process operational data (for example invocation metrics and logs) under its own terms and privacy policy. The app does not write page content or personal data to logs.

## Personal data
Pages you scan may contain personal data such as user mentions. The app reads that data only to detect unresolved user references and does not keep it.

## Your rights
Because we do not collect or store personal data through the app, we hold nothing to access, correct or delete. If you have questions, contact us.

## Changes
We will update this page if the app's data handling changes. The date above shows the latest version.
